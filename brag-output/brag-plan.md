# Brag Plan: CitizenPulse BRICS

## What is this app?
CitizenPulse BRICS is a Digital Public Infrastructure platform that converts unstructured citizen voice and text signals in regional languages into explainable priority scores, spatial heatmaps, and executive cabinet policy briefs.

## The angle
Deadpan startup launch for national infrastructure intelligence. We pitch a government AI command center with the slickness and swagger of a Silicon Valley Series A launch: "Governments receive millions of voice notes in 14 regional languages... so we built the AI priority engine for BRICS nations."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"14 Regional Languages. 500,000 Citizen Complaints. One AI Command Center."
High-contrast glassmorphic title card with cybernetic cyan glow slamming onto a deep blue slate background (`#07111F`).

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Multilingual Voice Ingestion**: Ingesting a rural Telugu drinking water voice request with 99.2% AI translation confidence.
- **Explainable Priority Score**: Dynamic 5-factor scoring gauge counting up to **91 / 100** (30% Demand + 25% Gap + 20% Population).
- **Cabinet Policy Brief & Simulation**: Auto-generating an executive policy brief and showing a 34% complaint reduction simulation.

## Outro / punchline
"CitizenPulse BRICS: From Citizen Voice to Infrastructure Intelligence."
Clean logo slam on dark glass panel with BRICS nation badges and hackathon submission URL (`citizenpulse.brics.gov`).

## User flow worth showing
Telugu voice signal received → Neural translation & 32% water deficit overlay → Explainable priority score 91/100 calculated → Executive Cabinet Brief generated.

## Tone
- Preset: `yc-parody`
- Creative direction: "Silicon Valley launch film for a BRICS national infrastructure command center"
- Interpretation: Serious, deadpan, hyper-confident presentation of high-stakes public infrastructure AI. Fast snappy cuts, clean cybernetic glass visuals, and zero generic fluff.

## Audio direction
- Role: Warm, steady business-tech bed with clean UI clicks, card entrance thuds, and a deep resonant logo hit.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: Starts at 0.0s at 0.28 volume, subtle dip during major text reveals, fade-out under final logo.
- Music cue guidance: Upbeat steady tempo (~118 BPM), snap key score reveal and logo landing onto strong beat drops.
- Audio-reactive treatment: Subtle cyan glow and glass panel border intensity breathe with music RMS/bass.
- SFX posture: Moderate, precise, motion-matched UI accents.
- Audio-coupled moments: Neural translation typing ticks, score gauge counting up 0 → 91, policy brief card pop-in.
- Restraint rule: No over-the-top sound effects or noisy arcade sounds; keep it crisp and executive.

High-impact opening. Dark background (`#07111F`) with animated cyan grid lines.

Subtitle: "Governments can't listen fast enough... until now."
Sequential/interaction: Text slams in fast with subtle scale (1.05 → 1.0), subtitle fades up 0.4s later.

Audio-coupled idea: Heavy soft impact on title slam.
Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at 0.28 volume.
Transition mood: Fast slide & crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Multilingual Signal Ingestion — 4.0s
Show the active Citizen Signal card.
Audio wave graphic representing Telugu voice memo → AI auto-translates: "Telugu (99.2% Conf.) → Drinking water supply shortage in Nalgonda Mandal."
Badge pops: "Population: 8,400 affected | Deficit: 32%".
Sequential/interaction: Voice wave plays, translated text types out, 2 data badges pop sequentially.
Audio intent: Show real AI work happening live.
Audio-coupled idea: Subtle keyboard ticks on text translation, soft card drop sound on badges.
Transition mood: Clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Explainable Priority Engine (91/100) — 4.5s
Show the Explainable Priority Score card.
Large glowing circular/stat gauge counting rapidly from 0 to **91 / 100**.
5-factor breakdown bars animate:
- Citizen Demand (30%): High
- Infra Deficit (25%): 32%
- Population Impact (20%): 8.4k
- Urgency (15%): Critical
Sequential/interaction: Stat gauge rolls 0 → 91, 4 score breakdown bars extend one by one.
Audio intent: The climax metric reveal.
Audio-coupled idea: Counter ticks on score count-up, resonant bell hit when 91 lands.
Transition mood: Hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Cabinet Policy Brief & Impact — 4.0s

Audio-coupled idea: Soft UI click and card placement sound.
Transition mood: Scale down & fade → Scene 5
